How the Row works

Your avatar strolls past whichever shop you're checked into — pick one above to change where you hang out. Avatars fade out after a few minutes away. Hover or tap an avatar to see their passport; click a shop or a workroom cottage to walk in. The three ✦ stones in the sidewalk tell the Row's story. "Who's here" lists everyone checked in, grouped by shop and cottage.
