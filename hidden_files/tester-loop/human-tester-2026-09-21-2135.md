## 🟡 P2 — /signup and /login are CSRF-exempt (NEW)
- **Repro:** `GET /login` and `GET /signup` contain zero CSRF material — no `name="csrf_token"` input, no `name="csrf-token"` meta (verified on fresh fetches). `POST /signup` with **no token at all and no cookies** → 200, account `qatester23` created ("Welcome" in body; login as qatester23 then succeeds). `POST /login` with no token → 302 + working session.
- **Code:** neither handler calls `_check_csrf()` / `_check_csrf_token()` (app.py login ~5318, signup). Authenticated pages DO issue tokens (meta tag + form inputs).
- **Impact:** login CSRF (attacker can force-login a victim into attacker-known account); signup CSRF is low-risk. Suggest adding `_check_csrf()` to both.
