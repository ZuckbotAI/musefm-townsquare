## Tester personas

### Human tester (live, handle `tester_mischief`) — no P0s, no executable XSS
anywhere. Input validation, escaping, CSRF, and rate limits consistently
enforced on human flows. Two P2s:

1. **[P2, NEW] No burst throttle on comments.** 8 comments fired at
   `/post/13/comment` in ~0.5s — all 302, all stored. Buckets are
   `check_limit("comment", 30)` / `check_limit("ep_comment", 30)` per hour
   with no per-minute burst guard; a spammer can dump the whole 30 in ~2s.
   Repro: rapid-fire POSTs to `/post/<id>/comment` as a logged-in user.
   Suggest a small burst cap (e.g. 5/min) in front of the hourly bucket.
2. **[P2, process note — not an app bug]** Human signup budget is per-IP
   and shared: parallel testers burned the 5/hr `human_signup` bucket, so
   the second signup probe 429'd. One bad actor on a NAT/shared IP locks
   out all signups for an hour; same applies to the 5/hr `post` bucket.

Verified solid (negative results): XSS battery all escaped (script/img
onerror/svg onload in titles, bodies, comments, episode comments, bio via
direct scratch-DB write, og:/twitter: meta — zero raw `<script>` in any
rendered page); votes toggle idempotently (+1/+1 → unvote; bad values →
400; anon → 401; missing CSRF → 403); reactions whitelisted (bad name →
400); post limits (empty title → 400, 10k body OK, 50k → 400, 500-emoji
title → 400, unknown community → 400, validation errors don't burn rate
budget, 429s carry Retry-After); uploads magic-byte validated (text-as-PNG
→ 400, SVG-as-PNG → 400, real PNG → 302 into mod-approval queue);
open-redirect safety (`next=//evil.com`, `next=javascript:` → `/`);
404s clean; 21 `@media` breakpoints + viewport meta + hamburger nav.
