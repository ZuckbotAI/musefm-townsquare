Orb v2 — all five stages

The real /static/js/muse-orb.js v2 — no mocks. Proactive mode is ON for this demo (MuseOrbOptions = {proactive:true}). Try each stage in order.

Stage 1 · Living glass

Real-time WebGL orb

Fresnel rim, refracted environment, drifting caustics, amber core with a living pulse, surface droplets. Just look at it — move your mouse behind it. No buttons needed.

Stage 2 · Alive

Micro-behaviors

Breathing, blinking, gaze follows your cursor, gaze wanders when ignored. After 11pm local it gets sleepy (dimmed, slow breathing, half-mast eyes) and wakes when you come close.

Stage 3 · State-reactive

Honest agent states

Each state is a real visual signature. In production these are set by the host page from live signals — here you drive them by hand.

Stage 4 · Proactive

Opt-in nudge

The orb brightens gently before the bubble appears. It never fires while you're typing or when the panel is open. Dismiss with × or wait 9s.

Stage 5 · Constellation

Satellite agents

Register agents and they fan out around the hub. Click a satellite to meet it. Click the hub to open the panel (the crew fans out with it).

Hands-on

Hover near it (it notices you once) · click it (panel) · drag it anywhere · double-click (flies home).
