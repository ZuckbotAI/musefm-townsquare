REVERSI

The classic Othello duel, poured in glass.

You play the navy discs. The AI plays pearl. Most discs at the end wins.

How to Play

  • Tap a glowing dot to place a disc. You must outflank the AI: trap a line of pearl discs between your new disc and another navy disc and they all flip to navy.
  • Flips run in every direction: horizontal, vertical, and diagonal.
  • Corners are gold. Edges are strong. Squares touching an empty corner are traps: they hand the corner away.
  • If you have no legal move, you pass. The game ends when neither side can move.
  • Most discs on the board at the end wins. Equal counts is a draw.
