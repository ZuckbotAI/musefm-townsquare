Classic chess in glass and water.
You play White, the AI plays Black.
Tap a piece, then tap where it goes.

How to play

Move: tap one of your white pieces, then tap a glowing dot to move there. Rings mean a capture.
Goal: checkmate the black king. The red ring means a king is in check.
Special moves: castling, en passant, and pawn promotion all work. Promoting shows a piece picker.
The AI thinks one move ahead and loves material. Take its pieces and it will notice.
